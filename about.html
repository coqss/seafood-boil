About Us

Joshua's Seafood Boil is a small, made-to-order business serving seafood boil in Pangasinan. Every order is prepared for the date you choose, which is why we ask for 3 days of preparation.

Choose your boil set, how spicy you like it, and your sauce, either garlic butter or sweet and spicy Cajun. We'll confirm your order by message and take care of the rest.
