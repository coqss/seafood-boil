Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 23, 2026

Joshua's Seafood Boil ("we", "us") respects your privacy. This policy explains what personal information we collect through this website and how we use it, in line with the Data Privacy Act of 2012 (Republic Act No. 10173) of the Philippines.

When you send the order or inquiry form, you tick a box to confirm you agree to this policy and our Terms & Conditions.

What we collect

  • Your name, mobile number and email address.
  • For orders: your chosen set, number of persons, spice level, sauce, delivery or pickup date, and delivery address (city or municipality, barangay and street).
  • Anything you type in the message box.

Why we use it

  • To receive, confirm, prepare and deliver your order.
  • To answer your questions and contact you about your order.
  • To keep a record of our orders and inquiries.

We do not sell your information and we do not use it for advertising.

Who can see it

Only us, and the online service providers that host this website and help us receive and organize orders. They handle your information only to provide those services. The address dropdowns load Pangasinan town and barangay names from a public Philippine place-names service (PSGC), which does not receive what you type in the form.

How long we keep it

We keep order and inquiry records only as long as we need them to serve you and to keep our business records. You can ask us to delete yours sooner.

Your rights

Under the Data Privacy Act you may ask to see the information we hold about you, have it corrected, object to how we use it, withdraw your consent, or have it deleted or blocked. To do any of these, contact us using the details on our Contact Us page. You may also file a complaint with the National Privacy Commission (privacy.gov.ph).

Security and changes

We take reasonable steps to protect your information, but no online system is completely secure. If we change this policy, the new version will appear on this page with a new date.
