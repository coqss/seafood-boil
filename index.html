Frequently Asked Questions

How far ahead do I need to order?

At least 3 days before your delivery or pickup date, since everything is made to order.

Can I change or cancel my order?

Please tell us as early as possible. Changes and cancellations may not be possible once we've started preparing your order. See our Terms & Conditions.

How many people does each set serve?

Set A serves 1–2, Set B serves 3–4 and Set C serves 5–6. For a bigger group, send us an inquiry.

Do you deliver outside Pangasinan?

Not at the moment. We deliver within Pangasinan only. Pickup is also available.

I have a seafood allergy. Is it safe to order?

Our food contains seafood, including shellfish, and we can't guarantee it is free from allergens or cross-contact. Please tell us about any allergies in the message box.

How do I pay?

We message you after your order to confirm the total and the payment options.
